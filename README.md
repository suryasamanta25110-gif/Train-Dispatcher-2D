# Train Dispatcher 2D

Train Dispatcher 2D is a 2D game where players are required to control train signals and track switches and deliver 
trains to their required destinations. The environment revolves around one particular station. Players will get option 
to choose which station they want to manage.

## How to play

The players can interact with only the signals and switches in-game.

- Turn signals to red if you want to make a train stop, or green if not.
- Turn switches accordingly to send trains to their required destinations.

Trains will stop at stations for a specific amount of time. Players will be given the list of arrival and departure of 
upcoming trains. The mission is to earn score while preventing accidents and delays.
